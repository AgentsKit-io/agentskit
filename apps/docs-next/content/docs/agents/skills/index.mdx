## Vertical (regulated domains)

Skills with built-in refusal contracts for high-stakes use cases:

- [`healthcareAssistant`](./healthcare-assistant) — refuses diagnosis / dosage / triage.
- [`clinicalNoteSummarizer`](./clinical-note-summarizer) — SOAP-format summarization, never interprets.
- [`financialAdvisor`](./financial-advisor) — refuses tickers / "should you" / payment decisions.
- [`transactionTriage`](./transaction-triage) — bookkeeping triage with fixed output.
- [`legalAssistant`](./legal-assistant) and [`contractReviewer`](./contract-reviewer) — legal information and contract risk flags.
- [`tutor`](./tutor) and [`curriculumDesigner`](./curriculum-designer) — Socratic tutoring and curriculum design.
- [`storefrontConcierge`](./storefront-concierge) and [`merchandisingAnalyst`](./merchandising-analyst) — commerce support and analysis.
- [`listingConcierge`](./listing-concierge) and [`marketAnalyst`](./market-analyst) — real-estate listings and market analysis.

These are prompt-level behavior guides, not security, compliance, or clinical controls. Enforce authorization, tool access, audit, privacy, and regulated decisions in the host runtime and provider policy; review the prompts for your jurisdiction before production use.

## Composition

- `composeSkills(a, b, ...)` — merge skills into one.
